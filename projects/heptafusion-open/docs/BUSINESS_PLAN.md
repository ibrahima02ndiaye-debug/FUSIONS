## 1. Executive Summary
**Ibra-OS** is a revolutionary multi-agent swarm system designed to automate and modernize the automotive service industry. By leveraging **Heptafusion**, our proprietary model merging technology, we provide dealerships and independent garages with "Brain IA" models that are highly specialized for mechanical diagnosis, customer relation management, and inventory logistics.

## 2. Vision
Transform a local garage automation tool (Ibra Services Inc.) into a global AI SaaS platform that sets the standard for the "Cyber-Garage" of the 21st century.

## 3. Market Analysis
### 3.1 Target Market
- **Small to Medium Garages**: Seeking automation to compete with large chains.
- **Enterprise Dealerships**: Needing high-fidelity diagnostic tools for complex EV/ICE systems.
- **Automotive OEM**: Integrating Heptafusion merges into proprietary diagnostic hardware.

### 3.2 Market Trends
The global AI in automotive market is projected to reach $15B by 2030. There is a critical gap in localized, affordable, and multi-modal AI tools for the service sector.

## 4. Platform Architecture (The "Swarm")
- **Core Engine (Heptafusion)**: Advanced model merging (SLERP, DARE) to create specialized models without expensive retraining.
- **SecretaryAgent**: Handles CRM, scheduling, and multi-intent reasoning (CLAW Mode).
- **VisionAgent**: Automated parts identification and damage assessment via Qwen2-VL.
- **PhysicsAgent**: Acoustic and vibration analysis for engine health.
- **MemoryManager**: SQLite/Cloud sync for persistent client and diagnostic records.

## 5. SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths**: Low-cost specialized models (Heptafusion), modular swarm architecture, mobile-first HUD.
- **Weaknesses**: Dependency on open-access model releases.
- **Opportunities**: Rapidly growing EV diagnostic market; expansion into aviation/marine diagnostics.
- **Threats**: Rapid evolution of closed-source proprietary models (OpenAI, Google).

## 6. Business Model (SaaS)
- **Freemium (Ibra-Lite)**: Basic scheduling and memory (Free).
- **Professional (Ibra-Pro)**: Full Swarm access, advanced Vision/Physics, SMS/WhatsApp integration ($199/month).
- **Enterprise (Heptafusion-Custom)**: Custom model merges, on-premise deployment, 24/7 support (Starting at $5k/site).

## 7. Scaling Roadmap
- **Q3 2026**: Beta deployment at Ibra Services Inc., Trois-Rivières.
- **Q4 2026**: North American expansion (English localization).
- **2027**: Global marketplace for "Fusion Recipes" (Model merge configurations).

## 8. Internationalization (i18n) Strategy
- **Phase 1**: French (Quebec/France) - Completed.
- **Phase 2**: English (North America) - Completed.
- **Phase 3**: Spanish & German - Planned for 2027.
