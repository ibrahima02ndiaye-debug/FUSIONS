# Marketing Plan: Ibra-OS / Heptafusion

## 1. Brand Identity: The "Cyber-Garage"
- **Brand Essence**: High-tech meets high-grease. We bring the power of silicon valley to the garage floor.
- **Visual Identity**: Neon green on black (Cyber aesthetic), terminal-style fonts, and sleek agent avatars.
- **Value Proposition**: "The Brain for Your Business. The Swarm for Your Shop."

## 2. Target Audience
- **The Modern Mechanic**: Tech-savvy shop owners looking for an edge.
- **Dealership CTOs**: Looking to optimize service department throughput.
- **Independent Technicians**: Using mobile tools for on-the-go diagnostics.

## 3. Channel Strategy
### 3.1 Digital Presence
- **LinkedIn**: High-level technical content focusing on Heptafusion merging capabilities for enterprise partners.
- **YouTube/TikTok**: "Cyber-Garage" shorts showing the Vision agent identifying parts and the Physics agent diagnosing engine knocks in real-time.
- **Official Website**: Interactive HUD demo and a "Fusion Playground" to test model merges.

### 3.2 Offline & Industry
- **Automotive Trade Shows**: SEMA, AAPEX. Live demos of the Ibra-OS Swarm in a mock-garage booth.
- **Partnerships**: Collaborative beta tests with local mechanical schools in Quebec and Canada.

## 4. Content Funnel
- **TOFU (Top of Funnel)**: Viral diagnostic videos and "The Future of Auto Repair" blog posts.
- **MOFU (Middle of Funnel)**: Whitepapers on "Model Merging vs. Fine-tuning in Automotive AI" and case studies from Ibra Services Inc.
- **BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)**: Free tier trial of Ibra-OS and custom quote consultations for Heptafusion Enterprise.

## 5. Global Launch Phases
- **French Launch (Quebec/France)**: Influencer campaigns with prominent Francophone car reviewers.
- **North American Launch**: English-language SEO focusing on "AI Mechanical Diagnosis" and "Garage Automation."
- **APAC Expansion**: Localization into Japanese and Korean to target major Asian automotive markets.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **User Growth**: Number of active "Ibra-Lite" users.
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of free users moving to Ibra-Pro.
- **Model Efficiency**: Average "AI Score" of diagnostics provided by the system.
