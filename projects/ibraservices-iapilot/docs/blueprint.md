# **App Name**: GaragePilot AI

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Car Diagnostic Tool: Enables users to upload symptoms and images of car problems for an AI-driven diagnostic. The LLM uses these to provide a probability distribution of most likely potential causes, and when to incorporate information in its output, making it act like a tool rather than just a chat assistant. The results of the diagnostic test get added to the vehicle record for later review.
- AI-Driven Predictive Maintenance: Analyzes vehicle history and current data using AI to predict future maintenance needs, displayed to the user within their portal.
- Dual Role Interface: Provides separate, tailored interfaces for garage staff and customers.
- Client Portal: Allows customers to view their vehicles, appointments, and approve quotes.
- Real-Time Messaging: Enables direct communication between the garage and customers, including an SOS feature.
- AI Chat Assistant: An integrated chatbot offers instant answers and assistance.
- Inventory Tracking: Manages parts, tracks stock levels, and sends low stock alerts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A saturated, modern blue (#4285F4) reflecting reliability and innovation, resonating with the diagnostic focus of the app. This color suggests trust and professionalism.
- Background color: A desaturated light-blue (#E3F2FD), visually very similar to the primary color, for a calm and modern backdrop.
- Accent color: A complementary violet (#90CAF9), offering contrast and drawing attention to key interactive elements.
- Body text: 'PT Sans', a sans-serif typeface to provide a neutral, readable experience that helps focus the user's attention on the AI analyses
- Headline text: 'Space Grotesk', a geometric sans-serif typeface to bring a touch of contemporary scientific styling to headings and labels
- Use clean, line-based icons to represent various garage functions and vehicle parts, ensuring clarity and ease of recognition.
- Incorporate subtle transitions and loading animations to provide a smooth and engaging user experience, especially during AI processing tasks.
